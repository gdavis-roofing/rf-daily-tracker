## Two things to know about the data itself

**1. The payment history is a reconstruction, not a transaction record.** 5,555 of
5,568 payment entries — **99.8%** — were created by the migration as a single summary
entry per invoice. They add up to the right totals on more than 99% of jobs, so the
books are sound. But if you open a job expecting to see individual cheques with
numbers and dates, you will find one lump entry instead. **The real cheque-level
detail still exists in AccuLynx**, and we can pull it for any job on request.

**2. Nine older completed jobs, $181,935, were paid in AccuLynx and never invoiced in
Eaveside.** These are 2021–2024 jobs whose cash was collected and banked years ago.
We are deliberately **leaving them as pre-Eaveside history** rather than raising
invoices now, which would create revenue entries dated today for work long closed.
Flagging it so it isn't a surprise; say the word if you'd rather we backfill them.

## What is not a problem, though it may look like one

Three things account for **$7.1 million** of apparent difference and are not errors —
the two systems are simply answering different questions:

- **Receivables.** AccuLynx counts the whole contract as owed the moment a job sells.
  Eaveside counts it only once an invoice is issued. Both are right.
- **Sales tax.** Our estimate totals exclude tax; AccuLynx's include it. Adding tax
  back ties 851 of 880 apparently-mismatched jobs exactly.
- **Commission.** Eaveside forecasts what a rep will be owed; AccuLynx records what
  was actually paid. We verified the paid amounts are all present in our cost records.
